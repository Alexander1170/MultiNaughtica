# Crafting from base and Cyclops lockers

Status: implemented with unit tests. The parts that depend on the real game objects (the scope ids of the lockers, the locker discovery, the claim round trip and the menu numbers) have not all been checked in a live game, so try it in your own world before you rely on it.

## What the player gets

- The crafting stations of a base (**Fabricator**, **Modification Station** (`Workbench`), the fabricator of a moonpool's **Vehicle Upgrade Console** (`BaseUpgradeConsoleGeometry.fabricator`)) and the **fabricator of a Cyclops** use the items in the lockers of their base / Cyclops as well as the player's inventory. All of them are a `GhostCrafter`, which is the one class the feature hooks. The upgrade console of a Cyclops (`UpgradeConsole`) does not craft in vanilla (it holds upgrade modules), so there is nothing to do there.
- The menu shows it: a recipe lights up when inventory plus lockers have the ingredients, and the counts in the tooltip include the lockers (`Quartz x2 (1)` with one Quartz in a locker and none in the inventory). The open menu listens to the lockers, so a recipe goes dark when the other player takes its ingredients.
- **Spend order: the inventory first, then the lockers.** From the lockers the items are taken in a fixed order (container id, then item id, ordinal), the same on every client.
- Scope is **the same sub root**: every `StorageContainer` under the `SubRoot` of the station (lockers, wall lockers, waterproof lockers placed inside, the built-in lockers of a Cyclops). Not counted: the containers that planters, aquariums, trash cans, filtration machines and scanner rooms name in their `storageContainer` field (living plants and fish, trash, machine output; a planter's container may sit next to the planter, so the field is used, not the hierarchy), the storage of a docked vehicle (`Vehicle` ancestor), bioreactors / reactors (they do not use `StorageContainer`), and the model of the virtual Cyclops. The waterproof locker (`PickupableStorage`) counts.
- Survival only (`GameModeUtils.RequiresIngredients()`); the creative modes spend nothing and nothing changes.
- A server option **`CraftFromLockers`** in `server.cfg` (default `true`); the server puts it into `SessionSettings.CraftFromLockers` of the initial sync of every joining player. With it off the menu counts the inventory only and a click is vanilla.

## No double spend: the claim

The client cannot take items out of a locker on its own: the other player's copy of the same locker would still hold them. When the inventory alone does not pay for the craft:

1. The client picks the exact items (`LockerCraftingRules.CreatePlan`) and sends `CraftingClaimRequest(claimId, scopeId, itemIds)`; the craft does **not** start (`GhostCrafter_Craft_Patch` skips the vanilla method; the power is not taken yet).
2. The server (`CraftingClaimRequestProcessor` + `CraftingClaimArbiter`) decides under a lock. The scope must be a `BuildEntity` (a base) or a Cyclops (`PlayerParentRules.IsCyclops`): a player, a planter or any other vehicle as the scope would turn the exclusions round (the walk up from an item stops at the scope first). Every id must be an `InventoryItemEntity` whose parents lead to `scopeId` without passing through a `PlayerEntity` (somebody's inventory), a `PlanterEntity`, a `VehicleEntity` that is not the scope itself, or a machine that is no locker: the pieces that are part of the base itself (bioreactor, reactor, water park, filtration machine, upgrade console, moonpool: `InteriorPieceEntity`; the scanner room: `MapRoomEntity`) and the standalone modules `Aquarium`, `Trashcans`, `LabTrashcan` (by tech type). Lockers, wall lockers and fabricators are `ModuleEntity`s. `CraftingClaimResponse` is on the server's relay blacklist: a client cannot send an answer to another client. The server does not refuse a claim because the sender's `SubRootId` differs from the scope (that value can lag behind); it logs a warning, which names the wrong side when a claim is refused. All or nothing: it removes the items from the registry (`WorldEntityManager.TryDestroyEntity`, like an `EntityDestroyed`), sends `EntityDestroyed` for each to **everybody, the claimant included**, and then `CraftingClaimResponse(claimId, Granted)` to the claimant. Each item is checked once more right before it is removed; a failure half way (an item gone or moved, or a removal that throws) puts what was removed back (with its parent link and children). `ClaimAsync` holds the claims of different players apart until the removals and the answer of one claim are out, so the loser always hears about the removal before its own refusal.
3. A **granted** answer starts the craft (when the station is free, `inProgress` and `HasCraftedItem()` both false: another player's finished, uncollected craft is never overwritten): the vanilla `GhostCrafter.Craft` runs again (flag `granting`); `CrafterLogic.ConsumeResources` is patched to spend only the **inventory's share** (the lockers' items are already gone) and plays the icons for all ingredients like vanilla. The crafting station then behaves exactly like any craft (`OnCraftingBegin` metadata, power draw report, pickup). The menu: vanilla `Action` locks the menu and remembers the clicked node after a successful `PerformAction`; our craft starts after the click, so `uGUI_CraftingMenu_Action_Patch` remembers the node and `LockerCrafting.RestoreMenuLock` does the same step afterwards (without it the menu stays dead after every craft of 5.9 s or less, because `Crafter.OnCraftingBegin` disables its raycasts and only the `locked` state brings them back).
4. A **Taken** answer (the other player was faster; the answer lists the unavailable ids) is tried **once more** with other items if the lockers still hold enough (the removal of the winner reaches the loser before the answer: the order is the one of the server's sends on one reliable ordered channel), else the craft is **cancelled** with the message "Crafting cancelled: someone took items from the lockers first." Nothing was removed, nothing is lost. `Disabled` (the option is off on the server) switches the client's flag off and says so; `Invalid` says the items could not be reserved.
5. Nothing is lost after a grant either: if the craft cannot start any more (the inventory lost its share while the answer was on its way, the station lost its power or got busy) the claimed items are given back as fresh items (`CraftData.AddToInventory`, at the feet when the inventory is full) with a message. A claim whose answer takes longer than 15 s is given up by the next click on a recipe of a station of a base or a Cyclops (until then it stays pending and a late granted answer starts the craft normally); a late granted answer for a claim that was given up is refunded the same way.

Protocol cost: a claim only exists when the lockers are needed. A craft the inventory pays for is exactly vanilla (no packet, the check `CrafterLogic.IsCraftRecipeFulfilled` counts the same). Packets changed: the next zip is **coop.7**.

While the vanilla `GhostCrafter.Craft` runs without a claim (the inventory pays, or the station has no power) the lockers are not counted at all (`inventoryOnly`, reset by the finalizer of `GhostCrafter_Craft_Patch`): vanilla `ConsumeResources` would otherwise decide on counts it cannot pay for (a station right after an EMP has `HasEnoughPower` false but takes the power).

## Where it hooks (all `IDynamicPatch`, `NitroxPatcher/Patches/Dynamic`)

| Patch | Method | What |
|---|---|---|
| `GhostCrafter_Craft_Patch` | `GhostCrafter.Craft` (prefix, finalizer) | plan, claim, skip vanilla while waiting; the finalizer ends `inventoryOnly` |
| `CrafterLogic_ConsumeResources_Patch` | `CrafterLogic.ConsumeResources` (prefix) | spend only the inventory's share during a granted craft |
| `CrafterLogic_IsCraftRecipeFulfilled_Patch` | transpiler | `Inventory.GetPickupCount` becomes `LockerCrafting.GetCount` (which recipes light up) |
| `TooltipFactory_WriteIngredients_Patch` | transpiler | same replacement (the counts in the tooltip) |
| `uGUI_CraftingMenu_Open_Patch`, `uGUI_CraftingMenu_OnDeselect_Patch` | postfixes | the open menu listens to the lockers' `onAddItem` / `onRemoveItem` (`InventoryChanged` -> `SetDirty`) and stops at `OnDeselect` (where the menu removes its own listeners; `Close(receiver)` runs for every crafter and does nothing unless it is the menu's own) |
| `uGUI_CraftingMenu_Action_Patch` | prefix | remembers the clicked recipe node for the menu step after a delayed craft |

`LockerCrafting.GetCount` adds the lockers only while the open crafting menu belongs to a `GhostCrafter` (`uGUI.main.craftingMenu.client`), so `Inventory.GetPickupCount` itself and every other caller stay vanilla. The Mobile Vehicle Bay (`ConstructorInput`), the rocket constructor and the Habitat Builder never see the lockers.

Code: `NitroxClient/GameLogic/LockerCrafting.cs` (Unity glue: scope, counts, claim state, refunds), `LockerCraftingRules.cs` (pure decisions), `NitroxClient/Communication/Packets/Processors/CraftingClaimResponseProcessor.cs`; server `Nitrox.Server.Subnautica/Models/GameLogic/Entities/CraftingClaimArbiter.cs`, `.../Packets/Processors/CraftingClaimRequestProcessor.cs`; packets `Nitrox.Model.Subnautica/Packets/CraftingClaimRequest.cs` / `CraftingClaimResponse.cs`; option `Nitrox.Model/Configuration/SubnauticaServerOptions.cs` + `SessionSettings.cs` + `JoiningManager.cs`; language keys `Nitrox_CraftFromLockers*` (English only, the other languages fall back to English).

## The Habitat Builder: left for later

Building base pieces from the lockers does not fit cleanly. `Constructable.Construct()` runs every frame while the builder is held and takes **one item per step synchronously** (`Inventory.DestroyItem(techType, false)` inside the method; it returns false and rolls the progress back when the item is missing). A claim is an answer from the server some milliseconds later, so it cannot sit inside that call. It would need a different design: claim the whole remaining resource list of a piece when the player starts building (an "advance claim" with a pocket the build draws from, handed back when the player stops or looks away), plus the interplay with the existing building packets (`ModifyConstructedAmount`, the base build arbitration) and the refund on deconstruction. That is a feature of its own, not a small addition.

## Tests

- `Nitrox.Test/Client/GameLogic/LockerCraftingRulesTest.cs` (32): counting (sum, negatives, overflow), merging of recipe lines, inventory first, selection order (container, then item, ordinal; two clients pick the same items), exclusions, missing amounts, the claim size cap, `NextStep` (granted / retry / cancel, retry limit), `ShouldReturnClaimedItems`, the timeout.
- `Nitrox.Test/Server/GameLogic/Entities/CraftingClaimArbiterTest.cs` (33): granted and removed, removal order, Cyclops chain, the second claim for the same item, an overlapping claim takes nothing, a **parallel race of two claims for the same 5 items in opposite orders (barrier, 50 rounds): exactly one granted**, the delivery of one claim is out before the next is decided, items of another base / a player's inventory / a planter / a docked vehicle / a bioreactor / non-items, a parent loop, a player / planter / non-Cyclops vehicle / module as the scope (invalid, nothing touched), invalid requests (null scope, empty, duplicates, null id, over the limit of 64), an outcome that was never filled in is no grant, a removal that fails half way or throws or an item that moved in between puts the earlier ones back with their parent and children.
- `Nitrox.Test/Server/Packets/Processors/CraftingClaimRequestProcessorTest.cs` (10): removals to everybody then the answer to the claimant, a refusal sends no removal, the second of two claims is refused, the loser hears of the removal before its refusal (sends with a delay, two overlapping claims), the option off, invalid requests (answered, claim id echoed), a player as the scope, both packets through the serializer, the defaults (option and session setting true).
- `PatchesTranspilerTest`: both transpilers applied to the real game IL (no net change in size, the IL does change).
- `PacketsSerializableTest` covers the two new packets and the new `SessionSettings` field generically.

## Not covered / open

- Not all of it has run in a live game: a live check is the proof that the scope id (`SubRoot` / `Base` NitroxId), the locker discovery (`StorageContainer` under the sub root), the claim round trip and the menu numbers match the real objects. The server log says what it took (`crafts from the lockers of <scope>`), the client log has `[CraftFromLockers]` lines for every step.
- A battery (or any item with state) that is claimed from a locker and then refunded comes back as a fresh item (full charge). Only the refund path does that; the normal craft spends the item.
- The menu does not mention that an ingredient comes from a locker. The numbers are the combined ones.
- Gamepad: the menu is the same; untested.
- Other languages: the four messages fall back to English.
- The station must be inside a `SubRoot` that has a NitroxId; a fabricator in the lifepod (not a sub root) stays vanilla.
